# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want CertMuse discussions to be welcoming, practical, and safe for learners, educators, operators, maintainers, and contributors of every background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence without attacking people.
- Keep feedback specific enough to be useful.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and security boundaries.
- Help keep discussions accessible to people with different levels of technical experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, sharing credentials, or posting copyrighted examination material without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or close content that violates these standards, and may limit participation when necessary to protect the community. Report concerns through the private route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue.
